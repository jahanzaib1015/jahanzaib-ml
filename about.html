About me

AI student, former intern, and someone who'd rather ship a model that works on real data than one that only looks good in a notebook.

I build machine learning models that hold up on messy, real world data, not just clean textbook datasets. I'm an AI student at Ibadat International University, Islamabad, and a former AI/ML Intern at AI Lab 99.

My focus is Python-based machine learning and deep learning, backed by a solid programming foundation in C++, Java, and C, plus a strong grip on data structures and algorithms.

At AI Lab 99 I worked through the full ML pipeline: cleaning and preprocessing data with Pandas, NumPy, and SQL, running exploratory data analysis, and handling feature engineering like missing value imputation and one hot encoding. I built models with Scikit-learn and evaluated them with accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and ROC AUC, along with cross validation, threshold tuning, model calibration, and SHAP explainability — the parts that separate a model that looks good on paper from one you can actually trust.

Right now I'm deepening my skills in deep learning and computer vision, and sharpening problem solving through DSA practice. I'm looking for AI/ML internships and entry level roles where I can keep learning while contributing to real projects.
